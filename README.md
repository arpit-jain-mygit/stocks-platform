# stocks-platform

**Insights from the filings, not tips for the market.**

Ask questions about listed companies' financial filings (results, annual reports) - any market, English filings.
RAG over the filing PDFs: Gemini (vision, answers, embeddings), Qdrant (chunks), Supabase Postgres (metadata).

## Layout

| Folder | What | Deployed to |
|---|---|---|
| [`backend/`](backend/) | FastAPI app, PDF ingestion, query, eval sets, SQL migrations | Railway (root directory `backend`) |
| [`ui/`](ui/) | Angular web UI: company cards, details panel, chat (`/ask`) | Vercel (root directory `ui`) |
| [`docs/`](docs/) | How to run, architecture, data design, roadmap | – |

Start here: [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) (setup and commands) · [docs/features.md](docs/features.md) (roadmap)

Secrets live only in `backend/.env` locally (copy `backend/.env.example`) and in Railway / Vercel variables. Never commit them.

## Disclaimer

stocks-insights.ai helps researchers find information in company filings and other documents it has ingested. Every answer is drawn only from those documents and cites its source.

It does **not** provide investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and it never recommends buying, selling or holding any security, nor gives price targets or forecasts. Asked for that, it replies: *"I can't recommend buying, selling or holding any stock. I can help you research it from the filings..."*

Answers are generated by AI and can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always verify figures against the cited filing before relying on them. Past results do not indicate future performance. Do your own research, or consult a registered investment adviser, before making any investment decision.
